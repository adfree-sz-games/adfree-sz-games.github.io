Roblox Unblocked

Roblox normally needs its own app, which school and work computers rarely let you install. The servers on this page get around that: each one runs Roblox on a remote machine and streams it to you in an ordinary browser tab, so all you need is Chrome, Edge or Firefox and a steady connection. It is the same Roblox you know, with your own avatar, friends and games like Brookhaven, Adopt Me and Blox Fruits.




Start with Servers 9 to 12. Servers 1 to 8 are older hosts and are often offline.


Every server is run by a third party, so your network may block some of them and the first load can take a minute.











How to play Roblox here

  1. Pick a server above. Servers 9 to 12 are the most reliable.
  2. Wait for the stream to start. A spinner or a short queue is normal; a black screen after 30 seconds is not.
  3. Log in to Roblox when it asks, then search for a game or open one from your Home page.
  4. Press F11 for full screen so the mouse and keys go to the game rather than the page.
  5. If the picture freezes or the server drops you, go back and choose another server.

Roblox unblocked FAQ

Is this the official Roblox?

No. Sz Games is not part of Roblox Corporation. Each server is a third-party cloud host that runs the real Roblox app on its own machine and streams the picture to your browser, so the games, avatars and Robux are the same as on Roblox itself.

Do I need a Roblox account?

Yes, Roblox asks you to log in before you can join a game. Because you type your login into a server run by someone else, use an account you would not mind losing, and turn on 2-step verification in your Roblox settings.

Why does a server show a blank or black screen?

The server is down, full, or blocked on your network. Wait about 30 seconds, then go back and try a different one. Servers 9 to 12 are the newer hosts and are the best ones to try first.
